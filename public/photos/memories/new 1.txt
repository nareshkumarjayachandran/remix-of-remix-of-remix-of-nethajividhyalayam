🌐 How to Deploy to Hostinger
Log in to Hostinger hPanel: Navigate to your website's File Manager (or connect via FTP / FileZilla).
Open public_html: Go into the public_html directory of your domain.
Upload Contents of dist:
Upload the contents inside the dist folder (not the dist folder itself) directly into public_html.
Your public_html directory should directly contain:
index.html
.htaccess (already generated with React SPA routing & caching rules)
assets/
photos/, videos/, lovable-uploads/
manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, etc.
Done: Visit your domain in the browser to view the live site.
